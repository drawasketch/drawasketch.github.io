Privacy Policy

Your Privacy on Draw A Sketch

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

At Draw A Sketch, accessible from drawasketch.github.io, your creative freedom and personal privacy are our top priorities. Because our canvas requires no login, subscription, or installations, we handle data with a strict minimalist approach.

1. Drawings and Canvas Data

Everything you draw on our sketchpad is processed directly inside your web browser. Your pencil drawings, animal sketches, or flower illustrations are never uploaded to our servers, stored by us, or visible to third parties. When you download your art as a PNG, JPG, or SVG file, that transaction happens completely local to your own device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Draw A Sketch is completely free and requires no account creation:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, usernames, or phone numbers.
  • We do not track or compile identities regarding who creates what drawing.
  • We do not store your created image files anywhere outside your local environment.

3. Automated Web Logging and Analytics

Like most websites, our host server automatically collects basic log files. This standard technical data may include internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, internet service provider (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. This baseline diagnostic data is completely anonymous and used exclusively for maintaining app security, service stability, and optimizing page speed.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We may use local storage mechanisms to remember temporary session variables, such as your current active brush size, color selection, or palette preference, ensuring a seamless user experience when switching tools. Third-party services, such as analytics providers or occasional ad-network structures helping to keep this tool free, may place minimal cookies to evaluate traffic metrics. You can control cookie rules in your browser options.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may reference external links or specific guided tutorials. Please note that we do not control the independent privacy standards of external websites. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of any site you visit after leaving ours.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or technical queries about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our GitHub project channel or our primary site support routes.
